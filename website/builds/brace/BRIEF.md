# BRACE launch surface brief

Self-authored, not interviewed.

The owner was genuinely unavailable for an interview during this autonomous build. These answers are written in the product's established voice and are intentionally treated as assumptions in the release report.

## Eight interview answers

1. **Vibe and references:** Precise, private, calm, operational, humane. Reference the controlled interface tension of *Severance*, the legibility and restraint of Braun systems, and the layered information density of a good field notebook.
2. **Scroll journey:** Begin inside BRACE with fragmented context visible. Show a project becoming a durable memory. Follow its provenance into a decision, then hand the same evidence to several AI clients. Prove the workflows with real product captures. End at the first local command.
3. **Energy curve:** Quiet and recognisable at first. More deliberate as the local boundary becomes clear. Most intense at the provenance handoff. Settle into confidence through product proof. Finish calm and actionable.
4. **Feelings and remembered moment:** Recognition, control, comprehension, surprise, trust, readiness. The one remembered moment is seeing a single evidence-backed memory travel intact from a local source to every connected AI.
5. **One thing no other site should do:** Let the visitor operate a working provenance handoff. They can search a synthetic memory, inspect its evidence, and switch the receiving AI while the same local memory packet visibly keeps its source intact.
6. **Aesthetic range:** Dense and editorial, one step away from premium-minimal. The interface should feel like a serious instrument with air and hierarchy, not a sparse luxury landing page or a maximalist spectacle.
7. **World structure:** Distinct operating states inside one continuous product surface. The cuts are changes of mode, not changes of visual world.
8. **Existing assets:** The final BRACE mark, palette, application icon, and real 1440 by 960 product captures made from the synthetic Northstar demo profile. No generated imagery and no personal data.

## Product brief

- **What it is and who it is for:** BRACE is a local-first personal AI memory layer for developers and knowledge workers who use more than one AI and are tired of rebuilding context in every conversation.
- **What the visitor must believe:** One private, provenance-backed memory can serve every AI without putting a cloud service in the middle.
- **The one next action:** Get BRACE.
- **Art direction:** A live software surface in deep ink, graphite, cool white, and a single acid-lime signal accent. Actual interface data and captures carry the visual proof. No generated world is required.

## Authored silence

There is one short resolved hold at the end of Act 3 after the packet reaches the selected endpoint. It exists so the visitor can read the evidence receipt before the surface advances. No other dead span is intentional.

## Grammar and score

- **Grammar:** Live surface. BRACE is software and its working surface is the argument.
- **Why the other grammars lost:** Filmic and worldflight would replace proof with spectacle. Split-stage would reduce the product to a before-and-after claim. Cutlist and chaptered editorial would make the interaction secondary. Type-led and typographic poster would hide the evidence model. Spatial canvas would weaken keyboard and small-screen comprehension.
- **Nav treatment:** Real compact app chrome with state tabs and a persistent local-status line.
- **Hero device:** A pinned, working recall surface populated from labelled synthetic data.
- **Act sequence:** `pin` → `flow + in` → `pin + custom provenance baton` → `reveal + pointer + parallax` → `flow + in`, five acts, 9.5 desktop viewport-heights and 9.1 mobile viewport-heights.
- **Close pattern:** An actual command field with copy feedback, plus guide and release links.
- **Signature move:** The provenance baton. A memory packet visibly preserves its source while the visitor changes the receiving AI.

| Beat | Device | Why this one |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition | `pin` | The product holds while an actual local search result assembles from data. |
| Boundary | `flow` + `in` | Quiet document movement gives the privacy model room to be understood. |
| Handoff | `pin` + bespoke `--sc-p` state | The peak needs controlled, inspectable state changes and the longest span. |
| Proof | `reveal` + `tilt` + `parallax` | A state wipe changes the active real capture while pointer response makes the evidence viewer operable. |
| First run | `flow` + `in` | The surface stops performing and becomes a practical first step. |

The score uses five device families, never repeats a family consecutively, includes no scrub video, contains no invented counters, and stays within the live-surface bans.

## Release verification

- **Automated scroll pass:** 28 desktop frames, 27 mobile frames, and 28 reduced-motion frames. Every frame settled, no dead scroll was detected, and all five acts remained readable.
- **Contact-sheet review:** Desktop preserves the live operating-surface composition; mobile becomes a legible single-column sequence; reduced motion keeps the same information and resolved close without depending on animation.
- **Keyboard and accessibility:** The launch page and How-to page passed keyboard focus, labelling, overflow, console-error, and axe WCAG 2.2 AA checks at desktop and mobile widths. Boundary, handoff, proof, and close states were checked independently.
- **Feel-check diff:** Cold-scroll feelings were curiosity, control, comprehension, trust, and readiness. These match the intended curve except that the handoff reads first as comprehension and then surprise, rather than the reverse. No visual change was needed because the evidence receipt is understood before its cross-client movement becomes the peak.
- **Peak and resolution:** The handoff is the largest visual state change and longest act. The close holds on an actual clone command, release link, and beginner guide rather than fading away.
- **Assets:** All product captures come from the packaged Electron boundary using only the synthetic Northstar profile. No generated imagery or private source data is used.
