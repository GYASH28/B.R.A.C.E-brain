launch surface. It helps future contributors avoid repeating an earlier site's
skeleton when they change only its styling.

close, and signature interaction. A future build should differ from every row

## Reserved choices

BRACE 0.1.0 claims the live-surface grammar, compact app-chrome navigation,
pinned working-recall hero, five-act 9.1–9.5vh band, command-field close,
provenance-baton interaction, and dense software-instrument world. It is the
first entry, so no earlier BRACE site constrained it.

BRACE 0.3.0 claims the split-stage grammar, vertical glass rail, live
forget/remember hero, five-act 13.8vh sequence, collapsing package-dock close,
provenance-lens interaction, and Arctic Glass memory-film world. It shares the
same local-memory subject with 0.1.0 but changes all six structural axes.

Rows are append-only after publication. Record what a new surface shares as
well as what it changes.

BRACE 0.7.0 shares the local-memory subject and a divided opening with 0.3.0,
but changes the grammar, navigation, act count, close, signature interaction,
and visual world. The real product panorama remains because it is evidence,
not decoration.

BRACE 0.8.0 keeps the real product panorama from 0.7.0 at the user's request,
but changes the other five structural dimensions: reel grammar, one-bar
navigation, scrub-film hero, four-beat sequence, flat installation close, and
the reversible frost-clear interaction. It differs from every earlier row on
at least four of six dimensions.
